Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Story Shorts Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner with the owner's own TikTok account.

Information collected

  • From TikTok: the OAuth access and refresh tokens, the account's open_id, and the scopes granted (user.info.basic, video.upload). Basic profile information is used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Upload status: TikTok publish IDs and processing status for videos the tool uploads.

The tool does not collect information about other TikTok users, followers, viewers, or their content.

How information is used

Only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok drafts and to check their upload status.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted (AWS Systems Manager Parameter Store, SecureString) in the owner's private cloud account. They are never shared, sold, or written to logs.

Sharing

No information is sold or shared with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok through its official API.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; the stored tokens are then deleted.
