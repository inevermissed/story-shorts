Story Shorts Uploader

Story Shorts Uploader is a personal, non-commercial tool. It creates original short story videos (AI-assisted script and narration) for its owner and uploads them to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. The account owner authorizes the app once with TikTok Login Kit.
  2. The tool generates an original vertical video.
  3. The video is uploaded to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  4. The owner reviews the draft in the TikTok app, applies the AI-generated content label, and decides whether to post it.

The tool does not post on behalf of anyone else, does not read other users' data, and does not use browser automation.
